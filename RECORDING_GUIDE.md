# 🎬 PAPER PERFECTOR DEMO - RECORDING GUIDE

**Total Time: 45 seconds**
**Target: Professional, efficient, crisp**

---

## 📋 BEFORE YOU HIT RECORD

### Setup (5 min)
- [ ] Close all other apps (email, slack, etc.)
- [ ] Open Paper Perfector in browser
- [ ] Have a messy text sample ready to paste:
  ```
  this is my paper about machine learning.
  it's not formatted well. lots of typos here.
  i need to make it professional. can this tool help?
  based on research from smith (2023) and jones (2022).
  ```
- [ ] Set up screen recording (Mac: Cmd+Space → "Screen Recording", or use OBS)
- [ ] Position camera if showing your face (optional but adds credibility)
- [ ] Phone on silent
- [ ] Close notifications (do not disturb mode)

### Recording Software
**Option A: QuickTime (Mac)**
```
1. Cmd + Space → "Screen Recording"
2. Click "Options" → Select which screen + mic
3. Click "Record"
4. When done: Stop, save to Desktop
```

**Option B: OBS (Better quality)**
```
1. Open OBS
2. Add Display Capture source
3. Set output to 1080p, 30fps, H.264
4. Click "Start Recording"
5. When done: File > Show Recordings
```

## 🎥 RECORDING SHOTS (Follow Exact Timing)

### Shot 1: LANDING PAGE (0:00 - 0:08) [8 seconds]

**What to do:**
1. Open Paper Perfector landing page (full screen)
2. Speak clearly, directly to camera (if visible) or just to mic
3. Show enthusiasm—this is your pitch

**Voiceover (Speak naturally, ~3 sentences):**
> "Formatting academic papers is a waste of human intelligence. Stop fighting with Word. This is Paper Perfector—your document engine for academic and professional writing."

**Screen:** Full Paper Perfector UI visible
**Audio:** Your voice + optional background music (add in editing)
**Timing:** Natural pace, don't rush

---

### Shot 2: MESSY IMPORT (0:08 - 0:15) [7 seconds]

**What to do:**
1. Click into the text editor
2. Clear any existing text
3. Paste the messy sample (Cmd+V)
4. Watch it appear in the editor

**Voiceover:**
> "Paste your messy draft directly into the engine. Notice the formatting is raw. No structure. Paper Perfector fixes this automatically."

**Screen:** Editor with cursor, paste happening
**Timing:** Show the paste happening, pause briefly to let text settle

### Shot 3: DARK MODE TOGGLE (0:15 - 0:20) [5 seconds]

**What to do:**

2. Click once to turn on Dark Mode

**Voiceover:**
> "A beautiful, distraction-free environment that respects your eyes. Dark mode native—perfect for late-night writing sessions."

**Timing:** Quick toggle, show both states

## 🔊 AUDIO TIPS

1. **Speak clearly** - Enunciate, natural pace
2. **No ums/ahs** - Speak like you own the solution (because you do)
3. **Confidence** - You're explaining your product to a buyer
4. **Volume** - Check levels before recording (use your phone to test)
5. **Quiet background** - No dog barks, no notifications

## 💡 PRO TIPS

1. **Do multiple takes** - Shoot 2-3x, keep the best one
2. **Natural pauses** - Let action settle before speaking next line
3. **Show, then tell** - Do the action first, voiceover explains second
4. **Confidence > perfection** - Slightly imperfect but confident beats overly polished and stiff
5. **End strong** - Your last words should emphasize the value (not a weak outro)
