Echo Sound Lab · Operator Guide

Friendly, Step-by-Step Music Creation

Non-technical guide for creators who just want to make music, finish songs, and publish confidently.

Profile: First Song
Mode: Friendly Wizard
Goal: Upload/Record -> Mix -> Master -> Video

1) Quick Start (2 Minutes)

  1. Open Echo Sound Lab and choose First Song profile.
  2. Record a vocal or upload your track on the Single Track page.
  3. Click the guided buttons in order: Analyze, Mix, Master, Video.
  4. Export your final file or queue exports in the background.
Guided flow: Record/Upload -> Analyze -> Mix -> Master -> Video

2) Which Profile Should I Pick?

  • First Song: most guided, best if this is your first time mixing/mastering.
  • Artist Fast Path: fewer prompts, faster turnaround.
  • Engineer Advanced: direct controls, logic-style workflow.

You can switch profiles anytime without losing your project.

3) Recording and Vocal Workflow

  • Pick your input device (USB interface, headset, Bluetooth, built-in mic).
  • Record multiple takes if needed.
  • Enable Smart Comping to merge strongest phrases from multiple takes.
  • Enable Honest Tuner for gentle pitch correction (musical, not robotic).
  • Use Vocal Textures when you want a stylized layer.

4) Mixing and Mastering (Plain English)

  • Analyze: finds what your track needs.
  • Mix: balances tone, dynamics, and clarity.
  • Master: final polish and playback safety.
  • What Changed: explains before/after without technical jargon.
  • Active Chain: shows exactly what was used.
If a process is blocked for safety, use Fix Blocked Mix to recover with safer settings.

5) Stems, Timeline, and Collaboration

  • Stem Splitter: create vocals/drums/bass/other when original stems are missing.
  • Project Timeline: rearrange sections quickly (intro/verse/chorus/outro).
  • Collaboration Panel: generate share links, leave comments, compare revisions.

6) Background Export Queue + Safe Updates

  • Queue WAV/MP3 exports while you continue editing.
  • When a new app build is available, choose Update Now or Later.
  • No forced kick-outs during active sessions.

7) Friendly Troubleshooting

  • No audio on mobile Safari: tap the on-screen audio resume button.
  • Buttons disabled: read inline reason text under the button.
  • Need support: use Copy Debug Info in settings and share it with support.
  • Recover work: Echo auto-saves and offers restore on relaunch.

8) Recommended Beginner Routine

  1. Start with First Song profile.
  2. Upload or record one track.
  3. Run Analyze -> Mix -> Master.
  4. A/B compare, then export WAV + MP3.
  5. Open Video mode and create content clips.
